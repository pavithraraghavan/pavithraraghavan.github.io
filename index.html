ABOUT

Research Profile

I am an M.Sc. Computer Science student at the University of Bonn, specializing in computer vision, generative AI, and intelligent systems. My work includes vision-language models, reinforcement learning, and flow-based generative modelling for 3D human pose estimation and motion forecasting.

For my Master's thesis in the Computer Vision Group at the University of Bonn, I am investigating flow-matching-based methods for recovering 3D human pose from monocular video, with an emphasis on input-informed initialization, multi-hypothesis prediction, and temporal forecasting.

Previously, as a Student Research Assistant at cciBonn, University Hospital Bonn, I worked on multimodal vision-language models for radiology report generation, including LlamaRG and InternVL-based approaches, and evaluated reinforcement-learning-based post-training methods.

My broader research interests include human motion modelling, dynamic 3D scene understanding, multimodal learning, and visual perception for autonomous and embodied systems, as well as applications of machine learning across scientific domains.

Current Research

MASTER'S THESIS · MAY 2026 – PRESENT

Generative 3D Human Pose Estimation and Forecasting from Video

Computer Vision Group · University of Bonn

Investigating flow-matching-based generative models for monocular 3D human pose estimation, including input-informed flow initialization and multi-hypothesis prediction.

Developing a video-to-3D pose estimation pipeline with the goal of integrating temporal motion forecasting and studying uncertainty and error propagation.

Evaluating 3D pose estimation on Human3.6M using MPJPE and PA-MPJPE, with comparisons to PHD and FMPose3D baselines.
