ABOUT

Research Profile

My work spans computer vision and generative AI, with experience in 3D human pose and motion modelling, flow-based generative models, vision-language systems, multimodal learning and reinforcement learning.

I am currently completing my Master's thesis in the Computer Vision Group at the University of Bonn, where I investigate generative approaches for monocular 3D human pose estimation and human motion forecasting.

Previously, as a Student Research Assistant at CCIBonn.ai, University Hospital Bonn, I worked on multimodal vision-language models for radiology report generation, reinforcement-learning-based fine-tuning, and large-scale PyTorch training and evaluation pipelines.

I am particularly interested in learning-based systems that can perceive, model and predict people, motion and dynamic visual environments, with broader interests in multimodal and embodied intelligence.

Current Research

My current Master's thesis research at the Computer Vision Group, University of Bonn, focuses on generative modelling for human pose estimation and future motion prediction.

MASTER'S THESIS · 2026

Monocular 3D Pose Estimation and Action Forecasting

Computer Vision Group · University of Bonn · May 2026 – Present

Investigating flow matching-based generative models for monocular 3D human pose estimation with probabilistic informed pose initialization and multi-hypothesis inference.

I am also exploring temporal skeletal representations for forecasting future human motion from monocular observations, with quantitative evaluation on Human3.6M.
