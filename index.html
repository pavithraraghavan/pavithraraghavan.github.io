About Me

Computer vision researcher and M.Sc. Computer Science student interested in learning-based visual intelligence.

I am an M.Sc. Computer Science student at the University of Bonn, specializing in Computer Vision, Generative AI and Intelligent Systems.

My current research focuses on generative approaches for monocular 3D human pose estimation and human motion forecasting. I am particularly interested in building models that understand people, motion and visual environments.

Previously, I worked as a Student Research Assistant at CCIBonn.ai, University Hospital Bonn, where I developed vision-language models for automatic radiology report generation and worked with reinforcement-learning-based fine-tuning.

Current Research

My current Master's thesis research at the Computer Vision Group, University of Bonn.

MASTER'S THESIS

Monocular 3D Pose Estimation and Action Forecasting

Computer Vision Group · University of Bonn · May 2026 – Present

Investigating flow matching-based generative models for monocular 3D human pose estimation using probabilistic informed pose initialization and multi-hypothesis inference.

I am also exploring temporal skeletal representations for forecasting future human motion from monocular observations and evaluating the approach on Human3.6M.

PyTorch Flow Matching 3D Human Pose Motion Forecasting Human3.6M OpenCV
